## What this skill does not cover

Design tokens, component styling, and responsive behavior — those belong to the
`ui-design-system` skill. Brand decides *which* colors and typefaces; the design
system decides how they're structured and applied.

## Standing checks

Apply to any name, mark, or palette before presenting it:

- **Legibility floor** — readable at 16px, and as a single flat color.
- **Category test** — could a competitor use this unchanged? If yes, it's generic.
- **Contrast** — brand colors must produce at least one WCAG AA text pairing. A
  palette that can't legally hold text gets overridden in implementation anyway.
- **Cross-cultural** — check names and symbols for unintended meaning in target markets.
- **Availability** — for names, check domain, existing trademarks, and social handles
  before presenting. Flag conflicts. You are not giving legal clearance; say so.

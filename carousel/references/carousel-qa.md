# Carousel QA contract

- Slides are 1080x1350 and readable at phone scale.
- Hebrew spelling and RTL order are correct.
- Meaningful text stays in safe zones and does not cover the focal object.
- One promise, one focal line, and one visual anchor per body slide.
- All text is rendered by Remotion, not generated inside images.
- Every non-original or AI-generated visual has source, permitted use, and
  required AI-disclosure recorded.
- Final assets contain no private metadata or unapproved personal information.
- Caption is final, in the business voice, and passed the copy gate when
  `israeli-social-content` is installed.
